Quick Start & Keyboard Shortcuts

How do I check off a to-do item directly on my Windows desktop wallpaper?

Keep NoteStrata™ running in your Windows System Tray (bottom-right clock area), minimize your open windows (`Win + D`), and left-click any checkbox on your wallpaper. It will toggle in ~20ms!

How do I quickly hide or show all sticky notes on my wallpaper before sharing my screen?

Press Alt + S from anywhere in Windows (or click the On Wallpaper button in the NoteStrata™ top bar) to toggle your wallpaper notes OFF and restore your clean original wallpaper. You can customize this shortcut anytime by clicking the Alt+S badge in the top bar.

Where are my notes and original wallpaper backup stored on my computer?

All NoteStrata™ files are stored locally inside your Windows user folder at %USERPROFILE%\.notestrata:
• notes_data.json & notes_data.backup.json (your sticky notes, archive history, and settings)
• original_wallpaper_backup.png (your clean, untouched original desktop wallpaper)

How do I restore a deleted note?

Click the History button in the top bar of NoteStrata™. Up to 40 deleted or cleared sticky notes are automatically archived locally and can be restored to your wallpaper with one click.
