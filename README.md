# NoteStrata™

> **Interactive Desktop Wallpaper Sticky Notes & Productivity Utility for Microsoft Windows**  
> *Created & Designed by Mohamed Dhia Eddine Mechi*

---

## 📌 Overview

**NoteStrata™** is a lightweight, local-first Windows utility that embeds interactive, draggable, rotatable sticky notes and task checklists **directly onto your active Windows desktop wallpaper**.

Unlike traditional note-taking apps that clutter your taskbar with floating windows, NoteStrata composes your notes directly into your desktop background image in real time. With its exclusive **Click-to-Check** feature, you can check off to-do items directly from your desktop surface without even opening the app window.

---

## ✨ Key Features

- 🖥️ **Direct Wallpaper Compositing:** Notes live directly on your wallpaper without floating windows or taskbar clutter.
- ☑️ **Desktop Click-to-Check:** Check off to-do tasks directly on your desktop surface in real time.
- 🎨 **Modern Color Studio & Typography:** Choose from 10 elegant typography styles, curated color palettes, custom HEX picker, and screen eyedropper.
- 🔄 **Angle Tilt & Free Dragging:** Rotate notes to custom angles, drag anywhere on your monitor canvas.
- ⌨️ **Global Visibility Hotkey (`Alt+S`):** Instantly toggle notes on or off on your desktop wallpaper from any application or game.
- 🔒 **100% Offline & Local-First:** Zero network requests, zero telemetry, zero analytics. Your notes never leave your personal computer.
- 🛡️ **Atomic Storage Protection:** Automatic backup fallback (`notes_data.backup.json`) guarantees your notes are safe from unexpected crashes or power outages.
